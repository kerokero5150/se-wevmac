ROM

Choose ROM…

A ROM you choose is read inside this page only. It is never uploaded, and it is gone when you close or reload the page. Use a 256K Mac SE ROM.

Disks

Insert disk image…

    Insert a .dsk, .img or .hda floppy or hard disk image. The Mac needs a startup disk. Changes are kept only until you close the page.

    Download saves an image with the changes you made. Eject the disk on the Mac first (drag it to the Trash, or choose Shut Down), or the copy may be incomplete. Images up to 512 MB.

    Click the screen to give it the keyboard. Hold Ctrl for Mini vMac's control mode.
