Kero's SE WevMac

Mini vMac for the Macintosh SE, in your browser. Start it with the stock ROM, or load your own SE ROM-inator image and boot from its ROM disk.

Mac SE Emulator Screen

ROM

Choose ROM…

A ROM you choose is read inside this page only. It is never uploaded, and it is gone when you close or reload the page. Use a 256K Mac SE ROM or a 1 MB SE ROM-inator image.

Disks

Insert disk image…

    Insert a .dsk or .img floppy or hard disk image. The stock ROM needs a startup disk; a ROM-inator image starts from its own ROM disk. Changes are kept only until you close the page.

    Download saves an image with the changes you made. Eject the disk on the Mac first (drag it to the Trash, or choose Shut Down), or the copy may be incomplete. Images up to 512 MB.

    Click the screen to give it the keyboard. Hold Ctrl for Mini vMac's control mode.
