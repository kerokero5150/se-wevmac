Kero's SE WevMac

Mini vMac for the Macintosh SE. Start it with the stock ROM, or load an SE ROM-inator image and boot from its ROM disk.

Checking the built-in ROM…

Mac SE Emulator Screen

ROM

Choose ROM…

Use a 256K Mac SE ROM, a 1 MB SE ROM-inator image, or the HI and LO halves of one (choose both files together). You can also drop ROM and disk files on the window.

Disks

Insert disk image…

    Insert a .dsk, .img or .hda floppy or hard disk image. The stock ROM needs a startup disk; a ROM-inator image starts from its own ROM disk. Changes stay in memory until you save the image. Before you press Start, Remove takes a ROM or disk back out; after that, eject disks on the Mac, or press Restart to begin again.

    Save writes an image with the changes you made. Eject the disk on the Mac first (drag it to the Trash, or choose Shut Down), or the copy may be incomplete. Images up to 512 MB.

    Click the screen to give it the keyboard. Hold Ctrl for Mini vMac's control mode. ⌘ shortcuts such as ⌘Q go to the Mac inside; quit the app from its menu.
